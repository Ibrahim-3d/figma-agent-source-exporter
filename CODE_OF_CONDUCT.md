# Code of Conduct

Figma → Agent is an open technical project. Participation should stay focused, useful, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and implementation details without personal attacks.
- Critique code, claims, benchmarks, and design decisions rather than people.
- Provide reproducible evidence when disputing fidelity or technical behavior.
- Respect confidential and proprietary design material.
- Do not publish another person's private source pack, client file, credentials, or personal information.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, or knowingly deceptive benchmark/result claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior materially harms the project or its contributors.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
